THE FIRST ACT / PROTOTYPE

A duel of reads.

Start with three basic moves in each of six color hands. Choose a connected room on the map and an opening color. Each fight has its own enemy moveset, visible in full.

  1. Choose one main from your active hand. Optionally commit a different card as a bonus and select any advantage your main can spend. Commitments reveal together.
  2. Alternate reaction burns or passes. Two consecutive passes resolve simultaneous damage: attack minus opposing block. Burns remove a card for this fight only; your main normally returns.
  3. Choose your next hand from your main’s routes. Your choice stays hidden until both are chosen. Entering an empty hand loses, as does losing all cards or reaching zero life. A simultaneous loss ends the run.
  4. Life persists between fights. Burned cards return, advantages and temporary effects reset. Rewards add cards to their printed color. Training permanently removes a card, keeping at least one in every color.

Read the life bar

Break: a one-use checkpoint stops incoming damage at its marked life value. Reaching it consumes it. Burst: the entire striped segment must be crossed in one exchange; insufficient damage stops at its upper edge. Damage above it still counts. Barrier: a separate pool absorbs damage before life. Pieces reset their protection each fight. An unused segment above your current life is inactive until healing reaches it; consumed segments never regenerate during that fight.

Confirmed damage: a life-damaging hit can mark damage for the following round. Connect through block again to add the mark before armor. Missing clears the old mark. Marks never accumulate; new marks are capped at 4.

Armor is a final damage limiter after combat and retaliation. Combat-only miss/retaliation checks use pre-armor combat damage; on-hit effects, exact-damage conditions, and objectives use final life damage. Recovery cannot revive a defeated fighter.

Equip one piece each in Head, Chest, Arms, and Legs between fights. New pieces auto-equip; old pieces stay in your pack. The run resumes in this browser’s PHP session. Death has no permanent upgrades.
