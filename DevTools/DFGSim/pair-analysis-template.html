Method and interpretation

    In the averaged view, each main-card cell averages every included bonus choice for both mains. That can hide strong counters: use the full-plan view or the exact plan selectors. The plan ranking gives more total weight to hands with more legal plans; it is a coverage summary, not a prediction of what a human will play. Reaction decisions, pre-owned tokens, depleted hands, hand-switch routes, and multi-round resource value can change the strategic result.
