## Paired card-removal experiments

For each selected card, replay the same tactical-bot opening, seed, and initial reaction priority with that card removed from seat 0's available pool. Positive change means having the card improved that player's score. Both seats have identical policies; seeds and colors are balanced across both initial priority settings. This removes all roles of the card, not only its main effect.

## How to interpret this

- High score **when used** is an association. Winners can reach certain hands more often, or save a card for a lethal turn. It does not establish that the card caused the win.
- Bonus marginal values hold the actual later reactions fixed; reaction values measure the immediate change before responses. Neither estimates long-term value of losing a reusable card.
- Tactical and casual profiles use the existing live bot with different move thresholds. Explore chooses a uniform legal main, a bonus with 25% probability when possible, and a uniform listed next hand; reactions still use the heuristic. Inspect profile-specific rates in summary.json before generalizing to human play.
- Card removals provide stronger evidence **for this bot and these matchups**, but change the bot's choices and resource pool. The bootstrap is an exploratory interval over balanced seeded matchup scenarios, with no multiple-comparison correction. A small interval away from zero is a lead for more testing, not a universal card-strength claim.
- Samples were used to choose removal candidates. Confirm interesting effects with new seeds before changing balance. Unused cards may be overlooked by the heuristic rather than weak. Stalled matches are not silently declared draws.
- Game rules and live bot/card data were not changed by this experiment. Raw JSONL traces record seeds, openings, plans, reactions, damage, life, and outcomes for reproducibility.
