Model and interpretation
  • Default: a uniformly shuffled four-card hand conditioned on containing a Bellow. Optional arsenal mode reserves one of the deck's Bellows in arsenal, then draws four from the remaining deck without conditioning. Setting up that arsenal in a previous cycle has no value assigned here.
  • Before attacking, choose which opening-hand cards to block with, maximizing expected block + damage while retaining a legal first Bellow. Block-3 cards contribute 3; nonblocks cannot defend. Assume enough incoming blockable damage to use every chosen block point. Blockers leave hand and cannot later be pitched, discarded, or played. Newly drawn and unused cards do not receive retroactive block credit.
  • The first action must be Bellow. Plays and pitches then maximize expected damage using only the visible hand and remaining deck composition. All legal pitch choices are explored, including pitching a non-six blue to protect discard odds. Equal values prefer the first enumerated line, with blue fives and nonblocking blues pitched first. Metric filters display components of the same full-cycle policy, rather than reoptimizing a different policy.
  • Bloodrush costs 1, pitches for 2, and grants +2 to every subsequent Brute attack, including weapons. Pay resources before discarding uniformly at random from the remaining hand. A six-power discard draws two without replacement and restores the action point. A miss still grants the buff but ends this model's turn.
  • Each Mandible Claw costs 2, attacks for 3, and can attack once. A prior six-power discard grants its go again. Abstract attack actions have no extra text or go again and end the sequence. Pitching stops when a cost is met; pitched cards cannot be drawn this turn.
  • Default fixed package: three copies each of four blue six-power block-3 cards and three blue six-power nonblocks (12 + 9). All are abstract Brute attacks. Blue costs are configurable; default 3 is an assumption, not a claim about every real card.
  • No hero abilities, equipment, opponent blocks, attack reactions, on-hit effects, or additional discard costs. Damage means unblocked attack power; full-cycle value = damage + pre-turn block. This abstract cycle value does not measure overall card balance or win rate.
  • One / two / three groups count Bellows actually played, including a final failed Bellow. Group frequency is conditional on a focused opening hand; mean and SD are conditional on that group. Empty groups display “—”. Failed discard means any Bellow missed six power. Min and max are exact reachable outcomes under the chosen expected-damage policy, rather than best-case choices made with hindsight.

Rules reference: Playing cards, §§5.1.7–5.1.9. This is a standalone abstraction; it does not execute FaBSim's full rules engine.
